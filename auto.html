Auto-Schedule Generator

The SIBT Intelligent Auto-Scheduler automatically compiles conflict-free timetables. It maps courses, rooms, teachers, days, and times using advanced heuristics that strictly enforce:

  • Teacher Availability: Preventing double-bookings.
  • Teacher Load Limits: Licensed (27 units), Non-licensed (24 units), Admin (9 units), Director (15 units), Program Head (18 units).
  • Room Compatibility: Laboratory courses automatically match lab/ComLab rooms, and lecture courses fit appropriately.
  • Section/Block Schedule: Student batches (like BSIT 1A) aren't double-booked.
  • Optimal Durations: Assigns time blocks based on standard durations (1, 1.5, 2, or 3 hours).
Pre-populate teachers, rooms, and subjects in the Manage Data tab before running!
